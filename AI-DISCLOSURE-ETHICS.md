This project was started as a fork of brepkit- and turned into its own independent project maintaining an Apache 2.0 license.

The upstream project brepkit's author was open on its use of AI tools- as such I retain such openness in this project.

All of my contributions are done through coding agents, PR review is done through agents etc.

The goal of this project for me is far too grand to be feasible without the use of AI tools- and while the ethics of how these tools were created can be debated (and in my opinion it has been the largest theft we will ever see)- the ability for a couple individuals to create a kernel usable in real projects- is something special- and I believe the license I uphold, or I hope at the least- an open license can justify usage of AI tools.

These tools were made from our collective knowledge so to will its output go- put to make up for the energy consumed I'm unofficially committing any profit this project may generate to clean energy initiatives- if no profits I'll just use my own money.
