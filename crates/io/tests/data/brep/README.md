# Saved-shape fixtures

Only geometry payloads are retained; archive XML and application metadata are
excluded. Trailing whitespace is stripped; all numeric geometry and topology
records are retained. No reference library is linked or executed by the tests.

- `freecad-box-v1.brep`: the geometry records from the `PartShape.brp` entry of FreeCAD's public
  `src/Mod/Fem/femtest/data/calculix/box.FCStd` at tag `1.1.3`.
  Source: <https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/blob/1.1.3/src/Mod/Fem/femtest/data/calculix/box.FCStd>.
  The source project is distributed under LGPL-2.1-or-later; this fixture is
  retained as third-party test data, outside the Rust implementation's license.
  It is a 10 mm cube with identity locations encoded both directly and as a
  composition. Expected volume: 1000 mm³; area: 600 mm².
- `box-v3.brep`: the `Body.brp` entry of OpenZCAD's synthetic
  `test/fixtures/freecad/two-solids.FCStd` at commit
  `a4543025652198bb226c1282b1af0c43e43e020e` (Apache-2.0).
  Source: <https://github.com/esaueng/OpenZCAD/blob/a4543025652198bb226c1282b1af0c43e43e020e/test/fixtures/freecad/two-solids.FCStd>.
  It is a 10 × 20 × 30 mm box. Expected volume: 6000 mm³; area: 2200 mm².

These are format fixtures, not a representative FreeCAD compatibility corpus.
These fixtures do not qualify general curved models, cavity shells or binary saves.

- `freecad-placed-box-v1.brep` and `freecad-cylinder-v1.brep`: the `Box.Shape.brp`
  and `Cylinder.Shape.brp` entries from FreeCAD's public
  [`data/examples/EngineBlock.FCStd` at tag 1.1.3](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/blob/1.1.3/data/examples/EngineBlock.FCStd).
  These remain LGPL-2.1-or-later third-party test data as above. The box is
  140 × 80 × 110 mm, translated by (0, −40, 0) mm; volume 1,232,000 mm³.
  The cylinder has radius 25 mm and height 110 mm, translated by (40, 0, 0) mm;
  volume 68,750π mm³, area 6,750π mm². Its left-handed cylinder frame, glued seam
  continuity token, translated reuse of a source rim/cap, and inverse location
  composition are retained exactly. The entire EngineBlock is not qualified.

- `tube-v1.brep`, `tube-v2.brep`, `tube-v3.brep`: synthetic controls written by
  `scripts/generate-brep-curved-fixtures.py`, with no CAD library. Outer radius
  5 mm, bore radius 2 mm, height 12 mm: volume 252π mm³, area 210π mm².
  Annular caps retain holes; the inner wall faces into the bore. Shuffled wall
  uses and space-separated seam continuity exercise both periodic branches.
  V2 includes four redundant cached UV endpoint values per surface representation,
  including paired seams. These caches are ignored, never acceptance evidence.

- `box-v2.brep`: a format control derived from the V3 box by writing the V2
  header and the four explicit UV endpoint coordinates for each linear pcurve.
  These redundant cache values exist in V2 only, not V1 or V3. Geometry and
  physical expectations are unchanged. This is synthetic format coverage.

The following synthetic V1 controls are generated by
`scripts/generate-brep-planar-fixtures.py`, using explicit oriented polygons
without a CAD library. They exercise multiple loops, including shuffled wire
edge uses and faces whose perimeter appears after their holes. They are not
claims of compatibility with additional authentic FreeCAD projects.

- `two-holes-v1.brep`: 10 × 12 × 8 mm block, with two 2 × 3 mm through-holes
  at XY rectangles [2,4] × [2,5] and [6,8] × [7,10]. Volume
  (120 − 2 × 6) × 8 = 864 mm³; area 2 × 108 + 44 × 8 + 2 × 10 × 8 = 728 mm².
  Four saved inner wires must survive transport, on the top and bottom faces.
- `blind-pocket-v1.brep`: the same block, with a 6 × 6 mm pocket from Z=8
  down to Z=5. Volume 960 − 36 × 3 = 852 mm³; area 592 + 24 × 3 = 664 mm².
  The top face has one inner wire; the pocket floor is a separate planar face.
- `concave-hole-v1.brep`: the block's XY perimeter omits its [6,10] × [7,12]
  corner and has the first through-hole above. Volume (120 − 20 − 6) × 8 =
  752 mm³; area 2 × 94 + 44 × 8 + 10 × 8 = 620 mm². Two inner wires survive.
- `oblique-two-holes-v1.brep`: the first control, with coordinates rotated
  about Z then X using 3–4–5 frames, and translated by (37, −19, 11) mm.
  The rigid motion is baked into geometry, with identity location references;
  it qualifies oblique plane charts, not nonidentity saved placements.
- `touching-hole-zero-v1.brep`: an intentionally invalid 10 × 12 × 8 mm block
  with a through-hole at [0,2] × [2,5], touching its perimeter. Source entity
  tolerances are zero; pcurve representations are omitted and the plane UV
  frames are rotated by a 3–4–5 rotation while 3D geometry stays axis-aligned.
  The new reader accepted this invalid solid before switching boundary
  classification to direct dominant-coordinate projection. It must refuse
  atomically, independently of the saved UV chart.
