The `issue_953_boa.json` fixture contains drawing commands for **Boa** in Open
Sans Regular, at an 8 mm em size and baseline `(10, 22)`. It was extracted with
opentype.js 2.0; implicit contour closures are recorded as `Z` commands. The
font is distributed under the accompanying SIL Open Font License. Tests use
only the saved drawing coordinates and require no font parser or font binary.

Independent polynomial Green's-theorem integrals, including the counters,
give glyph areas of 11.10082372029623, 7.022502263387043, and
7.090915044148765 mm². On the 62 × 50 × 10 mm slab, a 2 mm engraving therefore
has volume 30949.571517944336 mm³ and a 2 mm embossing has volume
31050.428482055664 mm³. The test bounds numerical property integration error
separately from the exact NURBS geometry.
