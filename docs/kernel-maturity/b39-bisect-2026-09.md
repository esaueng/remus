## 4. Mechanism on the current head (file:line)

Per the debugging-doctrine ladder (verify above AND below the gate):

- `remus_algo::gfa::boolean_with_context` returns `Ok` but the solid is a
  degenerate 1-face open shell (V=1, E=1, F=1, Euler=1, 1 boundary edge).
- The single-component acceptance gate in
  `crates/operations/src/boolean/mod.rs:1472-1479` (`euler_ok`, plus
  `open_shell_ok`, operand-representation, bounds, and
  `validate_boolean_result_with_tolerance`) rejects it; the multi-region
  gate at `mod.rs:1561-1578` cannot accept a 1-component result either.
- Control falls through to the mesh-fallback refusal point at
  `crates/operations/src/boolean/mod.rs:1679-1680`:
  `let FallbackPolicy::AllowApproximate { budget } = context.fallback
  else { return Err(OperationsError::ExactOnlyUnattainable); }`.
- Under `AllowApproximate` the same pair instead returns `Approximate`
  quality with 551 all-planar mesh faces, watertight at 0.1/0.01/0.001 of
  its own mesh — the mesh fallback, not the "handful of analytic faces +
  254 boundary" exact result the row describes. The row's numbers match
  neither the current exact path (refusal, no mesh) nor the current
  fallback path (551 faces, watertight).

Composition variants (move the torus instead of the cone; reversed
`Ry*T` order; operand swap) all refuse identically on this head, so the
cell refusal is robust, not a one-placement accident. (One out-of-scope
observation: the same shapes under `Rz(3π/2)` fuse `Exact` with 4 faces —
a trivially small overlap, not the reported crack; not pursued.)

## Appendix (2026-09-20): root cause traced below the gate; repair attempt reverted

Follow-up session on `b13ff97b` (origin/main), read-only investigation plus
one repair attempt that was reverted before landing. Method per the
debugging-doctrine ladder: exact carrier-curve residuals, per-pair section
dumps above the arrangement tracer, region ground truth by fine tube-grid
sampling.

**Why GFA degenerates.** For these placements the tube pierce boundary is a
*composite* loop assembled from three per-pair FF sections: cone-wall arcs
(the torus×frustum-wall carrier, ending at the cone's rim circles) plus
cap-plane ovals (torus×disk). The analytic marcher
(`march_analytic_intersection`) bounded its traces to the carriers'
parametric bands and stopped one full step short of the rim boundary — the
cone-wall arc's end sat ~0.02 from the rim∩torus crossing, a gap no
junction weld (1e-3 trigger, 1e-5 lookup) can absorb, so the composite
loops never chained, the arrangement tracer deferred, and the generic
paths misbuilt (unit: the torus face dropped, GFA degenerates to a 1-face
open shell, the gate correctly refuses; oblique: the two composite loops
concatenated as one malformed outer wire — 16 duplicated-edge entries,
orientation inconsistency both validators agree on, open mesh at fine
deflection, ~16% translation drift).

**Repair attempt (reverted).** Two coordinated changes were prototyped:
(1) the analytic marcher bisects its final step onto the band boundary
exactly; (2) `FaceExtent::outline_reach` lets both window emitters refine
bisected ends onto the true boundary curve for analytic extents too, and
whole open sections' endpoints resolve through the junction registry. This
made the oblique band emit (the wall arcs chained exactly), but it also
changed the endpoint behaviour of every analytic×analytic marched pair:
two WASM qualification cells that previously fused exact —
`batch_offset_torus_sphere_boolean_retains_exact_carriers` and
`batch_offset_cone_sphere_boolean_retains_exact_carriers` — began refusing
(their single-pair trims previously ended margin-short and were absorbed
downstream). The unit cell still did not green with the fix. Per the
qualification contract (existing exact cells must stay exact), both changes
were reverted; no kernel code changed in the delivered branch.

**Isolation follow-up (same session).** Re-testing each prototype piece in
isolation pinned the qualification regression to the CLOSED-window endpoint
snap alone — not the marcher bisection, and not the open-window
refinement: the whole-torus extent's boundary margin (TAU·0.01) made the
closed-window snap band ~0.06 wide, so the sphere/cylinder cells' interior
window ends (margin-short of the hemisphere equator / cylinder rim) were
eagerly snapped ONTO those rims, converting interior closed windows into
boundary-crossing windows and breaking the cap+band-with-hole splits that
those cells' exact results depend on. The open-window refinement plus the
restrict-tail junction resolution, on their own, left all probed
qualification cells bit-identical to main — but also left both B39 cells
unchanged (the wall arcs are kept whole, so no emitter refinement applies
to them), so they carry no standalone value and were not landed.

**Oblique structure ground truth (tube coordinates).** The region of the
tube surface inside the cone solid is a φ-wrapping band (the full tube
cross-section is swallowed near u≈348°; at u=0 the inside v-interval is
[0°,180°]; at u=336° it is [137°,271°]), so the B45 two-φ-wrapping-loop
band form IS the correct target. With the marcher fix the tracer receives
the cone-wall arc DOUBLED (two seed marches trace the same in-band arc
rim-to-rim; with exact endpoints they form a co-endpoint lens whose emit
collapses to a one-edge×two-traversal slit) and the cap oval WHOLE (its
rim crossings are not section endpoints). The remaining assembly work is
therefore: (a) suppress the duplicate wall trace at the section level
(same carrier, overlapping span), (b) split the cap oval at its exact
rim∩torus crossings so its arcs chain the wall arc into the composite
loops (the `emit_split_circle_arcs` analog for NURBS ovals), and (c) keep
the endpoints exact WITHOUT touching the closed-window path that the
sphere/cylinder qualification cells depend on.

**Remaining path forward (for the row owner):** the composite pierce loop
needs its sections' chain junctions to land on the SAME exact
rim∩torus crossing across the cone-wall pair and the two cap-plane pairs,
and the cap oval must be split at those crossings when it is emitted whole
(the closed-circle analog `emit_split_circle_arcs` provides for Circle
carriers). That is a phase-FF/builder-layer fix, not a marcher or tolerance
change.

## Appendix (2026-09-24): what closed it

Branch `fix/b39-toruscone-composite-loops`, based on `bdb44304` and merged
with `origin/main` (B33, #615) before the PR. Both witnesses green; no
tolerance was widened. The fix took six layers, found in pipeline order by
dumping each phase's output (section endpoints against the exact rim∩torus
crossings, splitter sub-faces, mesh per face). Each layer's first-wrong
evidence:

1. **Marcher band exit (math).** On `bdb44304` the unit cell's wall arcs
   ended 0.016–0.057 from the exact crossings R1..R4, e.g. `(0.5439, 2.4469,
   0.0809)` for R4 `(0.5, 2.46282, 0.11355)`. The oblique arcs ended 0.016 and
   0.019 from P1/P2. `march_analytic_intersection` now bisects the exiting
   step onto the band edge, and the ends land within 1e-13 of the crossings.
   The bisection is scoped to torus × frustum/cylinder-wall marches. Ungated,
   it broke two calibrated cells in the full suite:
   `fuse_overlapping_tapered_feet_welds_via_gfa` (a cone × cone end landed on
   the crossing of two coplanar cap rims and raw GFA went non-manifold) and
   `hammer_complete_opening_replay_preserves_dimensions_and_features` (exact
   ends of sphere × corner-round traces that run off the sphere patch
   survived the in-both restriction and wove an X across the round). A
   corner exit, which leaves both bands at one point, keeps the previous end.
2. **Cap ovals (phase FF).** `trim_torus_oval_to_box_face` now also serves
   disk caps: the rim∩torus crossings are found exactly (sign bracketing of
   the torus implicit along the rim, then bisection), and the kept arcs are
   chosen by the exact disk test. The sampled plane extent's margin was wider
   than the unit base oval's 0.03 bulge past its r = 1.5 rim. The unit cell
   raw GFA went from (1,1,1) to a strict-valid 4-face exact fuse at this step.
   The reverted prototype's closed-window snap was NOT used.
3. **One-rim wall notch (phase FF).** The oblique wall arc leaves and
   re-enters the top rim, so it and the rim span share both endpoints. It is
   split at its midpoint into two independent curves (`curve_split`), not
   two trimmed copies: the check crate reads a NURBS edge over its carrier's
   domain, and trimmed halves read as `WireSelfIntersection` (fixed at the
   source, filed separately).
4. **Periodic wall splitter (builder).** The greedy walker cannot trace a
   notched annulus: it closes on the first periodic-key return to its start
   and drops the far rim. It also discards the sector clear of the seam as a
   2π false closure, because the rims' pcurves unwrap in opposite senses.
   The unit fuse survived that (it keeps only the seam sector); the unit cut
   did not. `split_periodic_face_by_rim_chains` builds both configurations
   directly in the winding-chain band convention, gated to all-NURBS chains
   running between the rims.
5. **Plane×torus sampler (math).** The oblique base-cap oval started on a
   grid-corner near-duplicate pair (3.3e-4 apart, the second behind the
   start). The greedy chain doubled back, and the fit came within 2e-6 of
   itself at its seam. Both meshes folded there, giving 5 boundary and 2
   non-manifold edges at the harness deflection 1e-4. That is consistent with
   the fine-only openings on the holed plane that the diagnostics report
   measured (not re-bisected). A first-step reversal now swaps the pair.
6. **Torus notch-band mesher (tessellation).** Interior rows at constant
   ring angle, started past a wandering loop's u-spread (the oblique
   composite loop spans ~13°), left triangles 3.0e-2 off the tube at a 5e-4
   request. That was ~1 % of the intersect volume (1.0131 vs 1.00325). Rows
   are now ruled between the loops per tube angle, and the worst triangle is
   6e-5 at 1e-4.

Oracles on the final head: `crates/operations/tests/
regress_b39_toruscone_composite_pierce.rs` checks fuse/cut/intersect for
both cells. Every leg is exact, strict-valid on both validators, watertight
at 0.1 / 0.01 / fuzz-harness / B26-scaled deflections, and translation
invariant to 1e-5. Each volume is within 5e-4 of torus + frustum − I (fuse),
torus − I (cut) or I (intersect), where I is an independent semi-analytic
torus∩frustum integral: I = 0.3618160 (unit) and 1.0032475 (oblique),
converged to ~1e-6. Mesh-parity material accounting agrees at every sampled
grid point. The check crate's ray-cast classifier is NOT a usable oracle
here: it disagrees with analytic membership at ~5 % (oblique), ~0.3 % (unit)
and ~14 % on the already-landed B45 band fixture, i.e. it misreads trimmed
torus faces generally (filed separately).

## Appendix (2026-09-24, later): WASM smoke regression and fix-forward

#618 merged as `8539b266` and broke the `main`-only WASM Build & Validate
job. In `scripts/test-wasm-smoke.mjs`'s hammer-holder replay, the
`shiftedCommon` intersect refused with `ExactOnlyUnattainable`. The GFA
result had 104 faces but 4 free edges (Euler 11), and the gate rejected it.

The same sequence stays exact natively, including a native replay of the
WASM-computed operand bytes. So the operands were fine: wasm32 libm
rounding inside the intersect tipped a knife edge. Rebuilding the kernel
module once per toggle (`option_env!`, since WASM has no runtime env)
bisected it to layer 1 alone. With only the marcher's band-exit landing
off, WASM passes; with any other single layer off, it still fails.

In that intersect the landing fired on 12 marched torus-**fillet-patch** ×
cylinder traces (10 exits through the torus patch's own band edge),
putting their ends on patch-boundary corners. The fix narrows the landing
to a **full-tube** torus × wall march. That is B39's own family: the tube
band is whole, so a trace can only leave through the wall's rim. Fillet
patches keep their step-short ends, which the EF paves and junction
snapping absorb.

Evidence (WASM, hammer block up to `shiftedCommon`): the pre-B39 package
(`3fcb1bc4`) gives exact, 104 faces; the `8539b266`-lineage build throws;
the fixed build gives exact, 104 faces, 0 errors, watertight, with no band
exit taken. All B39 witnesses, the B45 fixture and the B39 regression file
stay green. `torus_patch_marches_keep_their_band_exit` pins the scope: it
fails under the merged gate and passes with the fix. Lesson: exact
endpoint landing on imported fillet patches is platform-sensitive, and the
native suite cannot see it, so any marcher-endpoint change needs the WASM
smoke (label `ci:full`) before merge.
