The slot played no part. At axes (10, 1) the cylinder with its top section 0.5 short of the slot misses the slot (2.3e-4 · s from its lower edge; the slicing integral's slot term is zero); the same cylinder through a plain plate came out wrong too, for every ellipse of axes (a, 1) from a ≈ 3.5, at every scale and plate thickness. Root: the established splitter left the cylinder's lateral whole without either closed section (`wire_builder` discards the loop), and `sections_left_out` (`algo/src/builder/face_splitter/mod.rs`), the check that sends such a result to the certified chart re-partition, gave every chord a slack of a tenth of its length, sized for the sagitta of a 64-sample curve chord; the lateral's seam is one line as long as the face, and a tenth of the steep cylinder's seam took in the section's midpoint 0.14 · s away from it. Fix: a seam (a line its wire runs up and back) gets no slack; the re-partition then runs and certifies 3 regions. Holding every line to the band was tried and refused `io/tests/hammer_opening_partition.rs` and `regress_hammer_opening_wasm_replay.rs` (a trimmed fillet whose established partition omits a section was sent to a re-partition that cannot certify it), so other edges keep the calibrated slack. Tests (`operations/tests/regress_shelled_wall_rim_notch.rs`): the witness is un-ignored and widened to top sections 0.5, 0.45 and 0.3 short (the last two enter the slot through its end wall below the top face) at scales 1e-3, 0.1, 1, 10 and 1000; `a_steeply_tilted_cylinder_through_a_plate_is_exact` covers axes (4, 1), (10, 1) and (100, 1) through a plain plate with the seam turned three ways at the same scales. Each case: slicing-integral or closed-form volume (`solid_volume` to 1e-9), face census (1 or 2 exact cylinders, the rest planes), validation, a watertight manifold mesh at two deflections with its enclosed volume within the deflection bound, and ray-cast material probes, cut and fused; at 1e-3 the only accepted outcome other than exact is `ExactOnlyUnattainable`. Both tests fail with the old slack. Residual, not chased: at scale 1e-3 (plate 5e-5 thick) the certified re-partition finds no interior point, because its chart clearance divides 100 tolerances by min(radius, 1) in both chart directions, and the public boolean refuses with `ExactOnlyUnattainable`; the (1000, 1) cylinder through a plain plate at scale 1000 is refused the same way (its chart certificate declines); the (1000, 1) slot cells with the slot's end 0.1 inside the section still refuse at scales 1 and 10 and are not this defect (the fix does not move them); the hammer fillet's omitted section is accepted as before, unexamined.
