and the operation journal. The bounded family is a full turn of a planar,
hole-free straight-edge radial profile through the existing analytic builder.
Partial turns and other profiles refuse the additive history API transactionally.
Result faces and circular rims carry construction provenance from the profile
face; reused seams and vertices retain their exact source identities. Actual
result F/E/V completeness is required before journal publication.

[`revolveRadialProfile`](https://github.com/esaueng/OpenZCAD/blob/d922a5b15ea72920101b6ec40ca6315950f056c3/packages/kernel-adapter/src/exact-cylinder-ops.ts),

slice supplies the native operation, native `Model`, direct `revolveJournaled`
and both batch routes. The consumer still calls legacy `revolve`; adapter
adoption, profile-edge-to-wall roles and reference continuity across complete
consumer feature rebuilds remain open. B18 remains Partial, including broader
revolve, sweep, loft and section attribution.

profiles across three scales and rigid placements, with independent volume/area,
raw mesh closure and byte-identical legacy geometry. Omission/phantom foils
prove result-set accounting. Real operation-output F/E/V references survive a
later pattern, a cylinder-cap move, checkpoint undo and edit replay. Refusals
preserve pre-existing topology and unpublished journal state, including curved,
holed and partial-turn controls. The direct/both-batch native binding contract
checks complete result sets and typed refusal parity; packaged consumer evidence
is required before delivery.
