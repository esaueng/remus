First slice (Tier: core — it returns live-session handles and their
evolution). `fuse_regions(&[SolidId])` (`crates/operations/src/boolean/nary.rs`) is a
new exact-only entry point; `fuse_solids`/`fuseAll` are unchanged and do not
route through it. Operands are grouped by conservative bounding-box contact:
one that can touch no other is returned as its own region with identity
lineage, and each larger group, which must be all-planar with straight edges,
is fused in one arrangement (`gfa::fuse_n_regions_with_entity_evolution`)
into regions in input order with every result face claimed once by an operand
face, total edge and vertex history, and a disposition per operand
(`Preserved`, `Contributes`, or `Absorbed` when the classifier placed every
one of its faces inside or on the others). Typed refusals
(`unsupported_nary_fuse` with a `reason`) are decided in the arrangement's
isolated store before anything is exported, so the live model and its arena
slot counts are unchanged: the same handle twice, a curved operand in a
touching group, more than 4096 touching pairs, an `Unknown` or unresolved `On`
classification (never counted as outside), an unrecorded result edge,
edge-only or vertex-only contact, and an arrangement or result the exact
engine cannot stand behind. A list holding a sheet or a wire refuses with the
existing `body_class_operand_unsupported`. Witnesses
(`crates/operations/tests/nary_fuse_regions.rs`, 24 tests) run axis-aligned and at
scales 1e-3, 1 and 1e3 under a rigid motion, with 2, 3, 5, 8 and 9 operands:
three boxes with no coplanar faces at the inclusion–exclusion volume 17.375, a
coplanar row (2), abutting cubes (3), partial-face contact, a far box, an
enclosed box, geometric twins, a staggered chain and a five-box overlap. Each
is held to an exact coordinate-compression union volume, a material probe in
every cell of the arrangement, manifold edge use by id and by position, a
position-welded mesh, strict validation and a geometric reading of every
face, edge and vertex lineage claim. Algo-level oracles
(`crates/algo/src/gfa/nary_oracle_tests.rs`, 11 tests) pin the store's caller maps,
the store-agnostic evolution export, the per-source face tally, the
pre-export gate and the refusals on hand-counted unions of unit cubes.
`fuseRegions(solids, sheets?, wires?)` shares one implementation between the
direct binding and `executeBatch`, and a contract test compares the two
payloads whole. Open boundary, pinned as a refusal witness: a nine-slab grid
whose top is a patchwork of coplanar faces from different operands is refused
(`lineage_unresolved`), because a seam the arrangement cut more than once
carries no construction record and faces of different operands share no input
edge that could name its parent; the single-solid fuse of the same slabs is
unaffected. `Absorbed` describes the operand's boundary, so an operand that
fills another's cavity is reported there too. Found, not fixed:
`classify_point` answers `Outside` for three of a plain `make_box`'s eight
corners, so the containment gate decides the boundary by certified distance.
