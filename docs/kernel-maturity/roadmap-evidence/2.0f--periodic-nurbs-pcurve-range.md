Closed edges on closed NURBS pcurves (#1832): OCCT writes sweep and
revolve cap pcurves as periodic rational B-splines whose domain starts half
a turn from the closed edge's vertex, and the reader trimmed them to the
empty range `(π, π)`, which the STEP writer then refused. The same collapse
hit a closed ELLIPSE whose cylinder pcurve runs u from 0 to 2π (closed only
modulo the period; `(3π/2, 3π/2)` on `iges_brep/ellipse_edges.step`). An
untrimmed NURBS pcurve of a closed edge that closes on its surface now
takes its whole domain, run the way the use leaves the vertex (the curve's
own tangent against the edge's analytic tangent, resolved in the canonical
chart on planes; coedge sense only as the fallback). When its seam is
elsewhere it is first re-seated at the vertex by exact Boehm knot
insertion (`reseat_closed_nurbs2d`, geometry unchanged; every vertex lift
inside the curve's control-point bounds is tried; degree and pole count
capped like untrimmed recovery and the refined copies charged to the
import budget), translating the moved arc by the closing period. A closed
edge's pcurve trace, derived or declared, must wind round the surface's
periodic axes exactly as the edge's sampled use does, so a loop once round
a cylinder is admitted and a loop round a torus's other axis is refused. An
empty derived range is refused at read time. Tests:
`step_periodic_nurbs_pcurve_range.rs` with the hand-written
`periodic_bspline_cap_cylinder.step` (fails on `main` with the issue's
exact `(3.1415926535895, 3.1415926535895)` writer error; full-domain caps
run the edge's way after two STEP round trips, also with one cap's control
net reversed; volume 1024π within 1e-6 relative; boundary authority valid)
and the ellipse file round-tripped twice at its own 942.477796; reader unit
tests pin re-seat geometry (rational circle, clamped cubic, period-shifted
loop, knots near 10^15), the use direction, the off-nearest-lift seam,
winding-checked declared and derived loops, the degree cap and the
empty-range refusal. The OpenZCAD `swept-arc-target.step` operand was
checked locally (not committed): it now writes and re-reads at 219.538848,
OCCT's reference volume.
