Sphere-seam re-blend (`resize_blend/sphere_seam.rs`): a cylindrical wall
whose seam with a sphere carries a rolling-ball fillet resizes with it in one
transaction (remove to the exact sharp seam, resize in the sphere-rim cell,
roll the measured radius back on). Coaxial torus bands stay exact
(`resize_blend` now accepts cylinder × sphere); off-axis sampled bands are
rebuilt at the source band's quality only under `allowApproximate`, disclosed
with measured envelope deviations. Walking-engine fixes put the off-axis seam
fillet on the correct side (descending-trim tangents, closed-band
orientation, seam-seated start). Native closed-form + rolling-ball band
volume oracles for r 6 → 5/7, axial and tilted, rollback and exact-only
refusal (`reblend_cylinder_sphere_seam.rs`); WASM direct/both-batch parity
(`resize_twin_reblends_the_sphere_boss_seam_fillet_with_permission`).
