The cone wall (one rim, a seam line to the apex, one pierce loop) is meshed by the developed-chart mesher `tessellate_revolved_with_holes` (`operations/src/tessellate/planar.rs`) through the snap path, since the apex fan and the two-rim band decline a face with an inner wire. For a pointed cone that mesher drops the doubled seam line from the outer wire and synthesizes the apex boundary itself, but only when `is_closed_full_turn_circle_rim` accepts the remaining rim as one connected full turn of one circle. That check compared the arcs' carriers bit for bit, and the fuse with the box rebuilt three of the seven rim arcs (the two either side of the seam vertex and one beside a box edge) on a circle of radius 2.0000000000000004 against the others' 2.0. The rim was declined, the doubled seam stayed in the chart polygon, and the mesh opened: at π/2 along the seam from rim to apex, at 0 and 1.0 rad round the whole pierce loop at deflection 1e-3. The box-free cone keeps bit-identical arcs, which is why the controls stayed closed; #1696's seam pre-split played no part. How the kept seam opens the pierce loop in particular was not traced: with the rim accepted the face never reaches that chart.

Fix: the check accepts arcs whose centres and radii agree within the linear tolerance (1e-7). The parameter frame (`u_axis`, `v_axis`) is still compared exactly, so the summed turn is read in one frame as before, and every arc's endpoints must still lie on its own carrier within the tolerance. Tests: the witness `operations/tests/regress_split_rim_cone_wall_pierce.rs::split_rim_cone_wall_pierce_mesh_is_closed` is un-ignored (closed, edge-manifold meshes at 0.1, 0.01 and 1e-3 for bars at 0, 1.0 and π/2 rad), and every closed mesh in it and in its controls must now enclose the exact body's `solid_volume` to within deflection × mesh area; the unit test `tessellate::planar::tests::native_apex_rim_accepts_split_carriers_within_linear_tolerance` accepts a rim with one arc an ulp off in radius and refuses one off by 1e-5. Residual, not chased: why the fuse rebuilds some split arcs on a carrier an ulp off.

Review fix (`gpt-6.2-sol`): a rim the tolerant check accepts can still spread over more `v` than the apex synthesis admits (one arc's carrier 6e-8 up the axis with its radius shrunk to stay on the cone, endpoints 6.7e-8 off it); dropping the seam then left the chart without its tip.
