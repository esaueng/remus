Budget-policy research (2026-11-13, regenerated 2026-11-14; measurement only,
no dispatch or geometry change). A test-only work probe
(`face_splitter/shadow/work.rs`, `perf-counters` builds, inert until
`REMUS_SPLITTER_WORK_LOG` is set; `scripts/splitter-work-census.py`), with an
input fingerprint per record, ran over every `remus-operations` and
`remus-io` test binary that logs a probe (221 binaries): 15,145 distinct inputs
in 917 tests. `march_steps` is the field that trips; the arrangement reads it
as one face's total charged work, and the smallest real split needs 376 steps
(d4 foil faces 838, honeycomb 1,317, largest split face 89,955 with 148 uses;
134 large faces also exceed `queue_size`). Work is quadratic in the collected
uses with a 2.7-21x spread that climbs with density. At default budgets neither
production adapter site ever split a face. The `budgets == WorkBudgets::new()`
gate was a live defect (raising `newton_iterations` by one turned three exact
cylinder booleans into mesh fallbacks), fixed by #1719. The disc and sector
emitters fire outside the foils (24 and 111 faces). The cylinder adapter's
traversal-order `trim` (fixed by #1719) made every band and sector shadow event
disagree; after the fix the sectors agree, while 860 band events still differ
in trim direction. Recommendation: route families explicitly (done in #1719),
add a dedicated `WorkBudgets::arrangement_steps` (default 2^20: 11.7x the
largest split, 7x the most work before a decline) and drop the arrangement's
use of `queue_size`/`segments`, then flip routing per family as each emitter is
deleted. Crossing-plane is ready (all 56 splits agree, 2 decline); the cylinder
flip needs the remaining band trim difference resolved and 29 refusals
triaged. The first census folded inputs on arena face IDs; its maxima stand,
and its counts moved as listed in the design note. Details:
`docs/design/uv-arrangement-splitter.md#o23d-budget-policy-measurements-and-recommendation-2026-11-13`.
