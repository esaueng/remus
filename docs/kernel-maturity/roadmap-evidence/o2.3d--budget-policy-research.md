Budget-policy research (2026-11-13, measurement only, no dispatch or geometry
change; decision pending). A test-only work probe (`face_splitter/shadow/work.rs`,
`perf-counters` builds, inert until `REMUS_SPLITTER_WORK_LOG` is set;
`scripts/splitter-work-census.py`) ran over every `remus-operations` and
`remus-io` test binary: 9,325 distinct faces in 895 tests. `march_steps` is the
field that trips; the arrangement reads it as one face's total work, and the
smallest real face needs 394 steps (d4 foil faces 838, honeycomb 1,317, largest
split face 89,955 with 148 uses; 130 large faces also exceed `queue_size`). Work
is quadratic in the collected uses with a 2.7-21x spread that climbs with density.
At default budgets neither production adapter site ever split a face. The
`budgets == WorkBudgets::new()` gate is a live defect: raising
`newton_iterations` by one turns three exact cylinder booleans into mesh
fallbacks (`ExactOnly`: an error), and raised SSI budgets reroute cylinder faces
through the adapter. The disc and sector emitters do fire outside the foils (6
and 69 faces). `arrangement_cyl` stores reversed-arc `trim` in traversal order,
which `traversal_domain()` misreads (confirmed on fixtures; every band face and
33 sector faces in the corpus). Recommendation: route families explicitly
instead of by budget equality, add a dedicated `WorkBudgets::arrangement_steps`
(default 2^20) and drop the arrangement's use of `queue_size`/`segments`, then
flip routing per family as each emitter is deleted. Crossing-plane is ready
(41 of 42 agree, 1 declined); the cylinder flip needs the trim fix and triage of
17 refusals. Details:
`docs/design/uv-arrangement-splitter.md#o23d-budget-policy-measurements-and-recommendation-2026-11-13`.
