Section-pave pass shipped (`crates/algo/src/pave_filler/section_paves.rs`, after `make_blocks`, both pipelines): on a section curve with a TRIMMED analytic curved face (hole wires, or an outer wire that closes in the chart and is not a rectangle) the trimming edges' pave vertices on the curve become extra paves (weld band 100·tol), the block is split by the splitter operand edges use (`make_blocks::split_at_extra_paves`), leaves off the face are dropped for both faces, and `build_section_map` feeds one section per surviving leaf with its `pave_block_id` (`IntersectionCurveDS::paved`). The in-repo repro had two further roots of the same shape, both fixed: the ray-cast classifier read a holed or bayed cylinder wall through the planar fallback or as a full band (the step-2 bore void classified INSIDE at 2,278 of 25,306 grid points; now `FaceGeom::ChartTrimmedCylinder`, outlines in `chart_trim.rs`), and no splitter joined a section to a hole or bay (`face_splitter/trimmed_cylinder_chart.rs`, a second front end to the certified chart tracer, run only where the established partition fails its own checks). Also: `dedup_collinear_sections` no longer reads a curved section's chord as a line. `crates/operations/tests/regress_b7_section_pave_attachment.rs` is un-ignored and green 3 of 3: wider half tunnel 4 bad edges / volume 319.757 → 0 / 308.385 (reference 308.387), oblique crossing 7 / 294.378 → 0 / 307.564 (reference 307.568), plus ray-cast material probes and an operand-classification grid. A vertex the section merely rides the boundary through is not paved (a cap circle along a herringbone patch's own rim arcs: `gear::cylinder::tests::internal_herringbone_teeth_in_a_bore_match_the_standalone_gear` moved 4e-6 in volume until that rule). Unit oracles: 14 closed-form cases in `section_paves.rs`, 4 in `chart_trim.rs`, 5 in `classifier/ray_cast.rs`. Not done: the split registry, boundary-vertex presplit and seam-split rules are untouched (curves between planes and chart rectangles are left exactly as phase FF made them); cones, spheres and tori with holes still take the classifier's old paths and have no chart partition; B8 can now read parameter-exact forward leaves from the pass but reversed sub-spans on closed curves are not attempted.
