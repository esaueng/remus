Seed `cylinder-cylinder/fuse/scale=0.001/placement=origin`: approximate on both, 16/50/36, volume 6.92e-7 on both. Cause of the two remaining cells: `stitch_rings` (`tessellate/nonplanar.rs`) joins a sphere cap's first interior row to its boundary ring by comparing longitudes that tie exactly. The boundary longitudes come from `atan2` in `project_point`, whose last bit differs between the Windows C runtime and wasm32's libm, so the comparison picked 16 quad diagonals of the sphere operand by rounding noise. A stage probe built for both targets from one source showed identical operand B-Reps and cone mesh, the first divergence in the sphere mesh's triangle indices, and the census gap carried through the mesh boolean. Fix (`3fd824c0`): longitudes within 16 ε of the larger one (about 1.4e-14 rad at 2π) tie and advance the lower ring; regression in `nonplanar/stitch_tie_tests.rs`. Rerun at `3fd824c0`: 0/366 cells differ, and serialized bytes are identical in 172/366 cells. The fix changed 13 cells identically on both targets, all disclosed-approximate fallbacks: volumes moved ≤ 7e-16 relative, face counts were unchanged, and 7 cells moved by 2 edges and 2 vertices. Logging every decision the tie band changes, across all 596 workspace test binaries: 153,728 flips in 225 tests, every gap ≤ 1.95e-14 rad. The geometry generation probe changes only `revolve/circle 120`'s mesh digest (counts and volume bits unchanged), and `approx_census` is unchanged. The test suites match baseline: the 11 failures on this host are pre-existing, all reproduce with the old comparison, and the 10 outside seed1146 hit no changed stitch decision. They are 9 CRLF-checkout fixture tests in `remus-io` and `remus-wasm-io`, `s04_tangent_converges` and the seed1146 cell below; `compute_mesh_lod` also crashed (GPU access violation). Volume bound: the matrix compared `|a - b| / max(|b|, 1)`, which is absolute below unit volume. At 1e-3 the "5e-8 relative" cross-surface bound therefore admitted 5–50 % disagreement, and the 1e-3 oracle bound admitted any answer.
