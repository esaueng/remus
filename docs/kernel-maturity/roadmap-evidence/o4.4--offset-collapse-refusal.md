Tier: core (the refusal rolls back live topology, journal and handles).
First typed offset refusal: an inward offset reaching a planar body's
half-thickness is `OperationsError::OffsetCollapsed` (additive variant
carrying the distance and, when certified, the thickness), wire code
`invalid_argument`, category `invalid_input`, `kernelCode`
`offset_collapsed` on `executeBatchV2` and `offsetDetailed`; legacy direct
and batch-v1 keep their message-only shape. An all-planar, straight-edged
solid is certified collapsed before construction when `2|d|` reaches its
thinnest face-normal slab (sound without convexity); the inside-out
postcondition is typed only for inward solid offsets. Closes P-Class 8.1
campaign seeds 10434068600042686585 and 3388039345656333454 (zero
thickness, was untyped `Topology(NonManifold)`) and 14788754164979531939
(pass-through, was generic `InvalidInput`), retained as
`crates/wasm/tests/repro/opseq81-box-offset-*.json` with native, harness,
batch cross-check and packaged regressions
(`crates/operations/tests/regress_opseq_offset_collapse.rs`,
`scripts/opseq-offset-collapse-packaged.mjs`). Outward offsets of boolean
results and other `AssemblyFailed`/`InvalidInput` errors stay untyped
tripwires. Gaps: curved bodies keep their per-face radius guards
(`InvalidInput`); a cylinder offset past half its height still returns `Ok`
(X8 stays out of generation; open ready-repro
`crates/operations/tests/regress_offset_cylinder_pass_through.rs`); non-planar bodies have no thickness
certificate; the rest of the registry is unchanged.
