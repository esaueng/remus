Torus × plane and torus × cylinder exact booleans on swept-tube and whole-torus
faces (OpenZCAD issues 1242 and 1244). Tier: core (GFA face splitting, phase
EF/FF and broad phase, editing the live solid/journal). Additive Rust APIs
`remus_algo::gfa::SectionDropScope` and
`remus_math::analytic_intersection::{VRangeHint, BandCoverage,
intersect_analytic_analytic_hinted}` carry diagnostics and coverage provenance;
the existing bounded intersection entry point keeps its contract. Torus faces bounded by iso-parameter
circles and closed in at least one direction are cut open along their seams
and partitioned as a planar arrangement certified on the exact curves
(`builder/face_splitter/periodic_face_chart.rs`, `chart_arrangement.rs`,
`chart_certificate.rs`: span enclosures from hull chart ranges and interval
velocity cones, bisected to a fixed budget; an admitted face that fails is
counted by `SectionDropScope::uncertified_charts` and refused by the exact
operation gates with transactional rollback); the
same arrangement re-partitions a planar face or cylinder lateral whose
established split runs a section out and back, leaves one out, or does not
tile the face (`plane_chart.rs`); admitted rescue failures are refused too.
The Boolean gate regression checks both failed-certificate counters against
a valid candidate with matching material and verifies transaction rollback;
the splitter's own crossing-section regression checks failure recording.
Whole-tube minor-angle bands, signed-distance
EF crossings with exact chart membership, rim-exact planar section windows and
a broad-phase box that keeps the whole carrier for any trim it cannot bound
exactly complete the sections. Reproducers:
`tests/regress_issue_1242_swept_tube_cut.rs` (cut, union and intersection
exact through every replay entry point, both orders, imported and
Remus-revolved tube, no dropped section chain; volumes within 0.005 mm³ of an
independent kernel at a 1e-9 integration tolerance) and
`tests/regress_issue_1244_torus_hole_intersect.rs` (imported and reloaded
operands exact in both orders through every entry point; the tessellated
volume extrapolates to the closed-form 570.78893 mm³ within 0.005). The
primitive torus bounded by degenerate seams is not yet qualified and still
refuses with rollback. `tests/regress_torus_section_loop_single_seam_cut.rs`
cuts a revolved tube with planes whose section loop crosses the tube's seam
once (it refused before); its volumes match closed-form and quadrature
references within 0.002 mm³ after mesh extrapolation.

A full minor-angle marching range is not proof of a whole tube. Bands carry
proved whole-tube, certified lifted, or uncertain provenance. Only proved
whole tubes enable rim-exact exits; certified wrap-straddling faces retain
their true periodic membership interval while marching a carrier superset.
Unknown membership is deferred on established clipping paths, never used as a
new endpoint-refinement certificate. The closed-window cone/cylinder repair
retains both face constraints and gives fit-noise credit only to a complete
non-degenerate rim analytically in the partner plane within the existing
linear tolerance. Torus windows retain their established refinement path.

A rejected chart candidate is rewound before a caller's explicit approximate
fallback runs. The policy regression requires exact-only refusal with rollback,
disclosed mesh quality and the declared budget when allowed, and no rejected
exact topology left live. Construction-history routes remain exact-only.

Circular sections that analytically retrace a real rim, with matching arc,
nodes and carried pave-block identity, duplicate a boundary rather than cut
it. Only those duplicates leave the chart; seam copies and real cuts retain
full certification. The original split-rim cylinder-resize target is covered
without changing its success, radius, bore, closure or volume assertions.

The left-out dispatch ignores a positive-weight NURBS section only when its
whole control hull proves it clear of face material by support separation
or containment in an authoritative full-circle hole. The declared linear
tolerance covers boundary contact; malformed hole domains and collapsed
separating directions do not certify clearance. Existing cylinder-gear
volume, diameter, flank-role and closure assertions stay unchanged.
