Tier: core. Chamfers now construct exact planar bands along straight ridges
between proven rational spline extrusion supports, and bounded ruled NURBS
bands on monotone planar spline rims with normal planar ends. Native
regressions cover rational/multispan and concave supports, 72 combinations of
placement, scale, orientation and asymmetric setbacks, curved top/bottom
rims, both rims together, retained distant bores, source preservation,
provenance and transactional refusals. Public batch regressions exercise
both envelopes and all chamfer modes. Independent STEP comparisons find no
material difference for four straight-ridge families; normal-offset and
section-integral oracles qualify the curved-rim fixture. Approximate rim
contacts carry a whole-interval bound inside the unchanged model tolerance.
The remaining families and work limits are recorded in
`docs/design/freeform-chamfer-construction.md`; this does not qualify arbitrary
doubly curved faces, spline loops or general interacting freeform corners.
