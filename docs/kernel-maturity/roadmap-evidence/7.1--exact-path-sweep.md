Exact path sweep along lines and circular arcs (2026-11-17, PR #1843, merge
`39f1b73c190f8dec397c907d3ce2efb3a70ce79a`, OpenZCAD M04 / CAD-003; Tier:
core): `exact_sweep::sweep_exact` and direct/batch `sweepExact(face, edges)`
carry a planar profile in place by the path's rigid motions (translation along
a line, turn about an arc's axis), which along a tangent-continuous chain is
the rotation-minimizing frame exactly. Each profile edge sweeps to one face per
segment: a plane, cylinder, cone or torus where one results, otherwise the
exact rational tensor product of the edge's NURBS with the motion. Corners
between two straight runs are mitred exactly (projected circles become exact
rational ellipses); a closed chain that returns the profile builds a capless
ring; path edges may come in any order and direction. `solid_volume` now also
reads bodies of planes and unholed four-sided curved windows (at most 512
faces, no sphere) off certified face integrals; the whole-solid mesh had read a
circle swept round a bend 5.7e-4 low. Tests:
`crates/operations/src/exact_sweep/tests.rs` (270° torus band, line–arc–line
bend, a triangle with a cone face against Pappus, an oblique line's exact NURBS
side, a holed tube, capless rings, a mitred L, round tube and closed square
frame against area × centre-line length within 1e-9, a 1 000-long near-axial
run kept off the cylinder, eight typed atomic refusals, watertight tessellation
of every surface family at deflections 0.05 and 0.01) and
`crates/wasm/src/bindings/exact_sweep_tests.rs` (direct and batch bend, a corner
refused atomically). Limits: only line and circular-arc path edges; a corner
next to an arc, or one sharper than 175°, is refused (`Unsupported`), as are
non-planar profiles, profiles reaching an arc's axis or lying along the path,
runs too short for their mitres, closed chains that do not return the profile,
and non-adjacent segments within twice the profile's reach. `sweep`,
`sweepWithOptions`, `sweepAlongEdges` and `helicalSweepInPlace` are unchanged,
so this row's `sweepWithOptions` qualification scope and its state are
unchanged.
